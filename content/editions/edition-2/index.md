The second edition of Ilhéu Magazine widens its lens from São Miguel to the full Azorean archipelago — nine islands scattered across 600 kilometres of open Atlantic, each with its own geological personality, cultural memory, and relationship to the sea.

From Corvo's 430 inhabitants — the smallest municipality in Portugal — to Terceira's UNESCO-listed Angra do Heroísmo, from the towering volcanic peak of Pico to the lush western gardens of Flores, this edition asks what it means to live in the middle of the ocean, connected to everywhere and belonging fully to nowhere.

Featuring new photography by Ana Costa, essays by João Medeiros, and a preview of Hun-Chung Lee's nine-island ceramic project, *The Nine* is a portrait of an archipelago at a crossroads — between tradition and tourism, isolation and connection, the deep past and an uncertain future.
