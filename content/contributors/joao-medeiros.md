João Medeiros writes about the Atlantic as a space of encounter — a place where European, African, and American currents have mixed for centuries, producing cultures that belong fully to none of these continents. Born in Faial to a family of whalers turned dairy farmers, his essays draw on deep personal roots in the Azorean archipelago.

His first book, *O Meio do Atlântico* (The Middle of the Atlantic), was shortlisted for the Prémio Literário José Saramago. He contributes regularly to Público, Expresso, and The White Review.
