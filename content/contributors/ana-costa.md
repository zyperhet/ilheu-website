---

Ana Costa's photography moves slowly. Her projects unfold over years, not weeks — extended meditations on place that resist the urgency of contemporary image-making. Based between Lisbon and Terceira, she has spent the last decade documenting the quiet transformations reshaping Atlantic island communities.

Her work has been exhibited at the Gulbenkian Foundation, the Centro Português de Fotografia, and Encontros da Imagem in Braga. In 2024, she was awarded the BES Photo Prize for her series *Depois do Silêncio*, which documented abandoned volcanic observation stations across the Azores.
