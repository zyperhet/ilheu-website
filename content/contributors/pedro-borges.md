---
name: Pedro Borges
role: Architect
bio: Pedro Borges is an award-winning architect whose renovation of the Terra Nostra Garden in Furnas, São Miguel, has been recognised as one of the most sensitive architectural interventions in the Azores. His practice centres on the dialogue between built form and volcanic landscape.
---

Pedro Borges approaches architecture as a conversation with place. Born in the Azores and trained in Porto, his work navigates the delicate space between preservation and intervention — asking what it means to build in a landscape shaped by volcanic forces over millennia.

His most celebrated project, the renovation of Terra Nostra Garden in Furnas, earned international recognition for its restraint. Rather than imposing a contemporary vision, Borges let the existing botanical landscape dictate the architectural language, creating structures that feel as though they grew from the garden itself.

"Architecture in the Azores must begin with humility," he says. "The landscape was here long before us, and it will outlast anything we build."
