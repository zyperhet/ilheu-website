---
name: Hugo Gonçales
role: Screenwriter
bio: Hugo Gonçales is a screenwriter from the Azores whose work on the Netflix series Rabo de Peixe brought international attention to São Miguel's fishing communities. His scripts explore the tension between tradition and modernity in island life, drawing on the landscapes and rhythms of the Atlantic.
---

Hugo Gonçales grew up in São Miguel, where the rhythms of the sea shaped his earliest stories. After studying film in Lisbon, he returned to the Azores with a determination to tell stories rooted in the islands — not as exotic backdrops, but as living, breathing characters in their own right.

His breakthrough came with *Rabo de Peixe*, a Netflix series that captured the raw beauty and economic struggles of a fishing village on São Miguel's north coast. The show became a cultural phenomenon, introducing global audiences to a world that had long existed outside the spotlight.

"The Atlantic isn't just scenery," Gonçales says. "It's a force that shapes how people think, how they relate to each other, how they understand time."
